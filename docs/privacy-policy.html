MuscleTrack T2D Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

MuscleTrack T2D is a health monitoring and self-tracking application. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease and does not provide medication-change recommendations.

Information entered into the app

Users may enter profile information, body weight, body-composition values, medication names and doses, physical-activity records, strength-training exercises, sets, repetitions, loads, goals and related monitoring information.

How information is stored

Version 0.1 stores user-entered information locally on the user's Android device. The app does not use a developer-operated cloud account, analytics service, advertising SDK or remote research database, and it does not transmit these health records to the developer.

Data sharing

Version 0.1 does not share user-entered health information with third parties. If future versions add optional cloud sync, Health Connect integration, research export or other external services, this policy and the in-app disclosures will be updated before those features are released.

Deletion

Users can delete locally stored app data using the data-deletion control within the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its app-specific local storage subject to Android device backup and operating-system behaviour.

Security

Information remains within the app's Android sandbox on the device. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device, screen lock and device backup settings.

Children

MuscleTrack T2D is intended for adults and is not designed for children.

Health disclaimer

The app is for monitoring and informational self-tracking only. It is not a medical device, does not make diagnoses, does not prescribe treatment and must not be used to make urgent medical decisions. Users should seek appropriate professional care for medical concerns.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Developer contact details will be provided on the Google Play listing. For repository-related enquiries, users may use the public project repository's issue tracker.
