MuscleTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 September 2026

MuscleTrack is a general workout-logging application.

Information entered into the app

Users may enter a name or nickname and workout information such as exercises, sets, repetitions, load, session duration, effort ratings and workout notes.

How information is stored

Version 0.3 stores user-entered workout information locally on the user's Android device. The app does not use a developer-operated cloud account, analytics service or advertising SDK, and it does not transmit workout records to the developer.

Data sharing

Version 0.3 does not share user-entered workout records with third parties.

Previous-version data

If a user upgrades from an earlier test version, older locally stored records may remain in the app database until the user deletes all app data or uninstalls the app. Version 0.3 does not provide interfaces for entering those retired data types and does not transmit them.

Deletion

Users can delete locally stored app data using the in-app deletion control. Uninstalling the app also removes app-specific local storage, subject to Android backup and operating-system behaviour.

Security

Information remains within the app's Android sandbox on the device. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and device backup settings.

Children

MuscleTrack is intended for adults and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change.

Contact

Developer contact details are provided on the Google Play listing.
